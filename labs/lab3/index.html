Rigatoni Alla Vodka


Rigatoni Alla Vodka was one of the first dishes my mom taught me how to make, it is very special to me due to this and it being an amazing dish that can feed me for days when I make it, it became my favorite dish with this sauce being used for multiple other things I make now especially chicken parmesean.

Ingredient Ingredient Amount
Rigatoni 1 Pound
Salt 1 Teaspoon
Olive Oil 1 1/2 Tbs
Tomato Paste 1/4 Cup
Minced Garlic 3 Cloves Minced
Yellow Onionl 2 diced
Vodka 1/4 Cup
Heavy Cream 1 Cup
Red Pepper 1 Tablespoon
Salt & Pepper To taste
